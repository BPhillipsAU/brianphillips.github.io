Delivering Complex Telecommunications Infrastructure & Large-Scale Migrations

I’m a Senior Project and Delivery Manager with a background that sits right at the intersection of technical engineering and commercial execution.

I started out in engineering and technical operations, spending years in the trenches of network consulting and incident management. That background taught me two things early on: exactly how complex systems break in the real world, and why protecting operational stability and service continuity has to come first.

Over the last decade, I took that technical grounding into formal project and delivery leadership, specialising in large-scale infrastructure modernisations, complex cutovers, and corporate separations. Most recently at TPG Telecom, I directed critical technical and commercial workstreams for the $5B divestment of their fibre assets and fixed-line business. That meant partnering with Tier-1 transaction advisors and legal teams to novate 33,000 contracts pre-sale, followed by collaborating with systems integrators to consolidate 15,000 enterprise accounts and nearly 40,000 services off legacy billing engines and networks. Throughout the separation, I co-chaired fortnightly Executive Steering Committees—delivering highlight reports and strategic updates directly to General Managers and senior TPG executives. Alongside corporate carve-outs, I’ve governed mission-critical deployments like migrating 700+ live network services powering Transport for NSW traffic systems, safeguarding public service continuity across live operational environments.

What defines my approach is being able to step into ambiguous, high-pressure environments, build governance and processes from scratch, and get cross-functional teams aligned on immovable deadlines. I don’t just track schedules; I focus on practical change enablement—holding partners accountable, preparing frontline teams for new technology, and actively limiting disruption while guiding clients through the realities of technical change.
